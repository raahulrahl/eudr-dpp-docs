# **I-Trace App Guidelines**

For Smallholders, Collectors, and Palm Oil Smallholder Groups

## **Chapter 1: Introduction**

## **1.About I-Trace**

PT Surveyor Indonesia and EFI, through the European Union-funded KAMI Project, developed the I-Trace supply chain tracking app. Although it was originally developed for the palm oil sector, I-Trace is adaptable for various commodities across Indonesia. The app specifically addresses the needs of small-scale agricultural commodity producers in Indonesia to ensure their products comply with legal standards and deforestation-free market requirements. Featuring a simple and user-friendly design, I-Trace enables producers to easily upload geolocation data and documentation of land legality. It facilitates effective record-keeping, monitoring, and verification of supply chain activities for all stakeholders, including smallholders, cooperatives, collectors, mills, and refineries. I-Trace is designed to be used in the field and the office, offering both mobile and web versions. The app provides an intuitive interface accessible to smallholders and other stakeholders, irrespective of their digital literacy levels.

#### **Key features of the I-Trace mobile app:**

- **Geolocation mapping**: Facilitates the mapping, uploading, and storage of geolocation information in production areas.
- **Transaction recording**: This feature enables smallholder cooperatives and other supply chain actors to record transactions, including the sales and purchases of agricultural commodities. It allows users to track payments, quantities, and other essential data.
- **Traceability**: This policy supports the traceability of products from source (smallholders) to export, including the transfer of information regarding legal and deforestation-free production.
- **Integration with national systems**: Interoperability with government and national systems (e.g., the National Dashboard and e-STDB platform).
- **I-Trace web interface**: The web interface simplifies data management with features such as bulk data upload for land registration and geolocation information. The dashboard and monitoring interface deliver valuable insights into resource transactions and trends. This feature is particularly useful for cooperatives and organisations managing multiple smallholders, as it enhances operational efficiency.

## **2. I-Trace Testing**

I-Trace was developed as a desktop version in August 2023, followed by the mobile app launch in October 2023. Testing was conducted in Karang Tunggal Village, Parenggean District, Central Kalimantan Province. Javlec Indonesia coordinated the testing, which involved technical experts from the European Forest Institute (EFI) and app developers from PT Surveyor Indonesia.

![](_page_2_Picture_2.jpeg)

The testing primarily focused on the palm oil sector, covering all processes from field to mill. It involved independent smallholders from Karang Tunggal Village, members of the Tri Daya Cooperative, collectors, and various mills within the supply chain. The testing was conducted in two phases:

- **Phase I:** The Alpha version of the app underwent field testing in February 2024 to evaluate core functionality and identify bugs and other issues.
- **Phase II:** The Beta version underwent field testing in October 2024. Based on feedback from independent smallholders, Tri Daya cooperatives, collectors, and mills, improvements and enhancements were introduced. Various supply chain actors subsequently utilised the app for two months. During this phase, PT Surveyor Indonesia and Javlec Indonesia provided external support to ensure the app operated effectively and to collect input for future improvements.

## **3.About the I-Trace Guidebook**

The I-Trace Guidebook is designed to support the utilisation of the I-Trace traceability app. It offers step-by-step instructions and equips users with the knowledge needed to navigate the app's features, from land registration to monitoring and verification.

It is a crucial reference for stakeholders, including smallholders, cooperatives, and businesses, who aim to adopt legal, deforestation-free, and traceable practices in their operations. The I-Trace Guidebook ensures that users—especially small-scale smallholders—can maximise the app's potential to enhance traceability, meet evolving market requirements, and expand access to global markets.

#### **5. Determining the geolocation of the plantation**

![](_page_12_Diagram_1.jpeg)

#### **a. Manual method**

The manual method allows users to enter plantation coordinates if they are available (e.g., from coordinates listed in the STDB document). This is a relatively accurate method for determining a location since the coordinates entered are the result of field mapping by GPS.

Each GPS coordinate is comprised of latitude and longitude coordinates. A minimum of 3 coordinate points is required. Additional coordinate points can be added as needed by clicking the "Add Location" button. Once all coordinate points for the plantation area have been entered, select "Save."

#### **c. Upload an SHP file**

Smallholders/users who already have polygon data in shapefile (shp) format can manually upload the data by uploading the plantation area shapefile to the app. The file must be saved as a ZIP folder in order to be uploaded.

Smallholders/users can map their land by directly locating it using the map displayed in the app and creating the plantation boundary points on the screen (click the boundary points on the map) to form a polygon representing the plantation area.

Note: The geolocations entered must be verified by the Admin to confirm the legality and deforestation-free status of the land before any transactions can be carried out.

The coordinate points of the plantation are essential for ensuring the traceability of fresh fruit bunches (FFB) during sales and purchase transactions, as well as deforestation-free and legality.
- 2. **Deforestation Free** (For example: 2/2) indicating that two out of two plantations registered in the app have obtained a deforestation-free declaration. This declaration aligns with the global policy established by the European Union's Deforestation Regulation, which prohibits deforestation during the land clearance of the plantation after the end of 2020. To obtain a deforestation-free declaration within this app, users only need to complete the plantation registration with the corresponding coordinate points. This will prompt the app developer team to conduct a verification process.
- 3. **Legality** (For example: 2/2) indicating that two out of two plantation plots registered in the app have met the legality requirements and are not located in forest areas. Similar to the deforestation-free status, users only need to complete the registration with coordinate points to obtain a legal declaration for the plantation registered in the app.

## **Chapter 3: Web-based desktop app**

![](_page_24_Picture_1.jpeg)

The web version of this app can be accessed via **<http://i-trace.sustainability.ptsi.co.id/login>**

While it may be less practical for field use, the web version is designed to assist supply chain actors working in an office, such as Group, Cooperative, and Mill Managers, in registering users more efficiently. The web version is particularly useful in the following scenarios:

- Supports cooperatives and smallholder groups in member registration through the 'bulk upload' function.
- Allows cooperative and group managers to view and monitor member transactions.
- Dashboard to view suppliers and off-takers within the supply base.

#### **1. Bulk upload feature**

The bulk upload feature is designed for smallholders who are members of smallholder groups or cooperatives. It allows users to input transaction data in large quantities at once.

Smallholder groups, cooperatives, or associations can facilitate the large-scale collective completion of member and geolocation data by facilitating the process.

The information required in the smallholder registration section—such as name, ID card number, address, land legality certificate number, STDB number, and geolocation information—is often already collected as part of the management of smallholder groups. The bulk upload feature simplifies the sign-up process on i-Trace for smallholders. It helps to reduce potential errors that may arise due to limited mapping skills among smallholders or users.

Cooperative group members can create accounts for all their members at once by entering the necessary data according to a **specially formatted table provided on the website.**

### **2. Download feature and transaction menu display**

In addition to the bulk upload feature, users of the web-based app can also download transaction data, particularly for smallholder groups and cooperatives. This functionality is very useful for recording members' transaction activities, enabling smallholder groups/cooperatives to track the number of transactions carried out by members over specified periods.

This supports smallholder groups and cooperatives in their efforts to obtain sustainable plantation certification, which requires transparency in the harvest data from member plantations. The transaction data includes transaction number, transaction date, truck number, seller name, off-taker name, and transaction quantity (in kilograms).

Moreover, users from Smallholder Groups/Cooperatives and Processing Mills can directly monitor transaction data in real-time via their computer screens for inspection and supervision.
