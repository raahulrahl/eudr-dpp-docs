# Surface area? Height? Forest cover?

Under both the EUDR and Cameroonian regulation, a 'forest' is defined as a minimum of 0.5 hectare, a minimum of 10% forest cover and a minimum tree height of 5 metres.

## 10 Cocoa must comply with the law

Under Cameroonian law, cocoa is not legal if it is produced in Forest Management Units (FMUs), communal forests and protected areas (national parks, forest reserves, etc.).
