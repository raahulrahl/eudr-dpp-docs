## Unit 2.3

### Enzymatic hydrolysis: Demonstrating mixed textile-waste recycling at pilot scale

*The enzymatic hydrolysis process described in Module 1 will be demonstrated at pilot scale within PLASTICE project. This includes a stepwise scaling up of the process from lab to pilot scale and the execution of a demo run, covering all steps from **textile sorting**, to the **chemical processes** to separate the fibres and recover the materials, to **yarn spinning** and fabric production. The entire consortium entrusted with the topic of textile recycling is working together on the demonstration of enzymatic recycling textile waste.*

#### **Key points of Unit 2.3**

- • **5.8 million tons** of textile waste are discarded annually in the EU, with most ending up in landfills or incinerators.
- • **Only 1%** of textile waste is recycled back into textiles; **25% is reused or downcycled**.
- • Modern clothing blends **multiple fibres** (e.g., cotton, polyester, wool, silk, synthetics), making recycling complex.
- • Effective recycling requires **breaking down mixed fabrics** into their components.
- • In PLASTICE, **enzymatic degradation** of cotton/polyester blends is used to recover polyester.
