#### 2.1. PFAS Waste Management Market

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) are a group of chemicals that have been widely used due to their unique properties, such as resistance to degradation in the environment, water and oil resistance, heat resistance, and nonstick capabilities. They have been used in many industrial applications and consumer products, such as nonstick cookware, stain-resistant fabrics, water-resistant fabrics, food packaging, and firefighting foam. This group of man-made chemicals includes many individual chemicals, with perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and perfluorooctanesulfonic acid (PFOS) being the most well-known. These compounds have raised concerns due to their persistence in the environment, as they are considered persistent organic pollutants (POPs), and have potential health effects.

To address the potential environmental and health concerns associated with PFAS, safer alternatives are being examined and efforts are being allocated to the improvement of detection and remediation methods

for contaminated sites. In particular, the global PFAS waste management market value was estimated at € 1.67 billion in 2022, growing with a CAGR of 5.2% to reach € 2.69 billion by 2031[1].

![](_page_9_Diagram_3.jpeg)

Figure 1. Global PFAS Waste Management Market 2023-2031 [1].

Governments in many countries have updated the regulations regarding PFAS waste management and disposal. The Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) and other regulations have implemented strict restrictions on PFAS compounds to protect the environment and human health. This is expected to be a key driver for PFAS waste management market growth. As public awareness is being increased regarding the risks associated with PFAS, the need for management solutions is also increasing. Governments, industrial key players and research institutes are collaborating to jointly develop new technologies and standards in this framework. They exchange knowledge and best practices, to upgrade the current PFAS waste management solutions and address faster and safer the risks.

Based on the type of PFAS, four main market segments can be identified, namely Perfluorooctane Sulfonate (PFOS), Perfluorooctanoic Acid (PFOA), Perfluorobutanoic Acid (PFBA), and Perfluorodecanoic Acid (PFDA). In 2022, the PFOA segment held the largest market share, while it is also expected to maintain its dominance during the forecast period 2023 to 2031. Despite its potential harmfulness, PFOA can be found in many applications, such as nonstick coatings, firefighting foam, and water-resistant textiles.

The PFAS waste management market consists of three primary segments based on the process used: landfilling, incineration, and chemical oxidation. Among these, incineration held the largest market share in 2022. This process aims to either break down the chemical compounds into less harmful substances or destroy them, by controlled burning at high temperatures. The method can manage many types of PFAS waste and is particularly suitable for large scale waste management purposes.

According to a regional analysis of the market, Asia Pacific is projected to dominate the market, primarily due to rapid industrialisation that is driving significant growth in the segment. Meanwhile, Europe and North America also represent noteworthy markets in the industry.

#### 2.2. Biopolymers market

Biopolymers are derived from natural, renewable sources, such as plants, animals, microorganisms, in contrast to traditional synthetic polymers that are derived from fossil fuels. They are considered more environmentally friendly due to their potential for biodegradability, decreased reliance on fossil fuels, and reduced environmental impact of plastic waste. They can be used in various applications, including food, pharmaceutical and biomedical industries.

While biopolymers are a broad category of polymers derived from natural sources, bioplastics are a subset of biopolymers that are used as an alternative to traditional petroleum-based plastics. The global biopolymers market size was valued at € 10.73 billion in 2022, growing at a CAGR of 15.79% to reach € 34.66 billion by 2030 [5]. The market growth of biopolymers will be driven by several factors, including their lower carbon footprint, reduced waste generation, improved compostability, and decreased energy costs. As consumer awareness is increasing, the demand for eco-friendlier, safer, and more sustainable, healthier solutions is increasing. Government policies and waste management regulations support the reduction of plastic waste driving the demand for biopolymers. At the same time, the use of biopolymers in the packaging industry is rising, as biopolymers producing biodegradable and compostable packaging materials that can reduce the environmental impact of packaging waste [6].

On the other hand, the market growth may be restrained due to the high cost of biopolymers production and potential performance issues that may restrict their penetration into specific applications. Guided by government policies, however, consumer goods, packaging and agriculture applications are anticipated to provide growth opportunities for biobased polymers. Special attention shall also be given to a remaining challenge that concerns the non-biodegradable biopolymers processing. It is also worth noting that two years after the coronavirus pandemic, the expected economic growth turned into slow growth and inflation, as new challenges appeared, such supply chain constrains and high oil and food prices due to the Russia-Ukraine war [6].

Based on the type of biopolymers, the market is segmented into Bio-PE, Bio-PET, Polylactic acid (PLA), Polyhydroxyalkanoate (PHA), Biodegradable polyesters, and others. In 2022, the biodegradable polyesters segment dominated the market and is expected to continue during the forecast period. Segment growth is driven by many applications of biodegradable polyesters in biomedical and medical equipment, packaging, automotive, consumer goods, and textiles industries.

#### 2.3.1. Organic coatings market

Organic coatings are thin layers of protective or decorative materials applied to various surfaces. They are widely used in industries such as automotive, aerospace, construction, and manufacturing to enhance the durability and appearance of substrates like metal, wood, plastics, and composites.

# 5. Market forces

Porter's Five Forces analysis is a strategic framework that provides a structured approach for analysing the competitive forces within an industry. It offers valuable insights into the attractiveness and profitability of an industry, helping businesses understand the dynamics and competitive landscape they operate in.

#### A. Threat of new entrants

The first force is the threat of new entrants, which examines the barriers to entry for new companies. It considers factors such as capital requirements, economies of scale, government regulations, and brand loyalty. A high level of entry barriers makes it difficult for new players to enter the market, reducing the threat to existing companies. Conversely, low barriers can lead to increased competition and lower profitability. Understanding the threat of new entrants helps businesses assess the sustainability of their competitive advantage and develop appropriate strategies to defend their position.

#### B. Bargaining power of suppliers

The second force is the bargaining power of suppliers, which examines the influence suppliers have over the industry. Suppliers with significant power can demand higher prices, favourable terms, or limit the availability of key resources. This can negatively impact a company's profitability. Factors such as the number of suppliers, uniqueness of their products or services, and switching costs determine their bargaining power. By analysing supplier power, businesses can develop effective supplier management strategies, explore alternative sourcing options, or negotiate favourable contracts to mitigate risks.

#### C. Bargaining power of buyers

The third force is the bargaining power of buyers, which assesses the influence customers have over the industry. Buyers with strong bargaining power can demand lower prices, higher quality, or better terms, putting pressure on industry profitability. Factors such as the concentration of buyers, their price sensitivity, and the availability of substitute products affect buyer power. Understanding buyer behaviour enables businesses to tailor their marketing strategies, enhance customer value propositions, and maintain strong customer relationships to minimise the risk of losing market share.

#### D. Threat of Substitutes

This force considers the availability of substitute products or services outside the industry. Substitutes provide alternatives that customers can turn to, thereby limiting the potential market share and profitability of existing industry players. The availability, price-performance ratio, and switching costs associated with substitutes affect their threat level.

#### E. Competitive Rivalry

This force focuses on the intensity of competition among existing companies within the industry. Factors such as the number of competitors, market growth rate, and product differentiation impact the level of rivalry. High competition often leads to price wars, reduced profitability, and the need for constant innovation and differentiation.

In summary, Porter's Five Forces analysis offers a comprehensive framework to assess the competitive forces within an industry. By analysing the threat of new entrants, bargaining power of suppliers, and bargaining power of buyers, businesses gain valuable insights into the industry dynamics, competitive intensity, and profitability potential. This understanding helps companies develop effective strategies to navigate the competitive landscape, identify opportunities for differentiation, and maintain a sustainable competitive advantage.

# 8. Conclusions

The current deliverable, D7.2, of the PROPLANET project has provided a comprehensive market analysis, focusing on the primary market landscape, specific sectors, competitors, and the project's strengths and weaknesses. This analysis has enabled the consortium to gain valuable insights into market opportunities and pave the way for the successful commercialisation of their innovative coatings.

The primary market landscape analysis explored various sectors, including biopolymers, coatings, organic coatings, and bio-based coatings. Of particular interest were the textiles sector, food packaging equipment market, and glass coating market. By understanding the current trends, demand, and growth potential in these sectors, PROPLANET has identified areas for innovation and development of coatings that meet industry demands.

The exhaustive market analysis for coatings in the textiles sector focused on coatings with oleophobic and water-repellent attributes, addressing the sector's need for durable and sustainable coatings. Additionally, coatings for food-packaging machinery and low-maintenance glass were examined, considering properties such as anti-corrosion, non-stickiness, high thermal resistance, and enhanced mechanical stress properties. This thorough analysis has guided the development of coatings that align with market needs and provide a competitive advantage for the project.

Furthermore, the upcoming competitors' analysis, as well as the SWOT and PESTLE analysis will provide additional insights into the competitive landscape, while they will further enhance the project's positioning and differentiation in the market, setting the stage for successful market penetration.

Additionally, the forthcoming examination of the patent landscape related to coatings will ensure that PROPLANET's innovations are novel and protected. By understanding existing patents and intellectual property considerations, the consortium can leverage existing knowledge and avoid potential infringements, strengthening their position in the market.
